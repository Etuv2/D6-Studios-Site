01 Overview

Overview

Build a deck of anime fighters. Take it into every kind of battle.

Open booster packs to collect character cards, then level them up and build a deck around the fighters you like best. In battle, every character brings its own skills and effects.

Your progress follows you everywhere, so the deck you build takes on the campaign, ranked matches, raids and dungeon floors alike.

02 Game modes

Ways to play

Seven modes in the current build. ArcVerse is still in development, so expect changes before launch.

  • Solo

    Campaign

    Travel from island to island, beating each island's opponents to unlock the next.

  • Ranked 1v1

    Arena

    Ranked one-on-one battles with your own deck, building rating across seasonal reward tracks.

  • Ranked 1v1

    Strategic Warfare

    Ranked duels where card order, Leader choice and matchup planning beat account power.

  • Solo

    King of the Hill

    Hold the hill against random enemy teams that grow stronger with every win.

  • Solo or party

    Raid Boss

    Take on a boss alone or with a party. Every hit and effect carries over to the next challenger.

  • Solo run

    Roguelike Arcs

    A different run every time: draft a deck from booster packs, collect relics and defeat three Act bosses.

  • Solo

    Dungeons of Eternity

    Clear three battles per floor and survive the stacking challenges of each floor's final fight.
