Alexandru (Mihai) Tunaru

Creative Developer & Designer

After completing a BSc (Hons) in Games Software Engineering at Bournemouth University, I am now studying for an MSc in Human-Centred Artificial Intelligence for Games Development, with a passion for creating immersive digital experiences. I specialize in game development (Unity, Godot/GDScript - Interlinked Initiative & Temporal Entropy were made in Godot), web applications, and 3D modeling, bringing ideas to life through code and creativity.

When I'm not studying or coding, you'll find me participating in game jams, contributing to indie projects, or exploring the latest technologies in C++, Unity, and web development.
